# embarch-core interfaces: Studies

**Status:** active, 2026-09-07. Split out of [../interfaces.md](../interfaces.md) on 2026-09-07 (`tasks/core/020`) when that file reached its size cap — a reference table, not cut, per [../DOC-COMPACTION.md](../../DOC-COMPACTION.md) §3.

Index: [../interfaces.md](../interfaces.md). Conventions and rationale: [../interfaces.md](../interfaces.md), [../decisions/studies.md](../decisions/studies.md), [../decisions/handshake.md](../decisions/handshake.md).

Async and job-based rather than blocking, unlike everything in the other route groups: a study's BLE steps can take unbounded time. One study in flight at a time via `study_lock`; no cancel endpoint; the in-memory job registry has no expiry and does not survive a restart. Design of record: [../../embarch-study-designer/decisions.md](../../embarch-study-designer/decisions.md) §5.1.

| Method | Path | Body / Query | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET` | `/dev-bench/hello` | — | `{schema_version, compatible, firmware_version, self_reported_hardware_id, probe_hardware_id, link_identity}` — opens the link just long enough to handshake, reports it, closes. **`firmware_version` here is the *bench's* build, and the field it corresponds to in a `Study` is `requires.dev_bench_version`, not the identically named `requires.firmware_version`, which is the DUT's** (`embarch-study-designer` decision 74). Writing this value into `requires.firmware_version` is accepted silently: that requirement is only compared when a run supplies `flashed_firmware_version`. `link_identity` is `match`/`mismatch`/`not-reported`/`undeclared`; both IDs are reported rather than only enforced, because reading them side by side is what writing a new chip's relation requires. `self_reported_hardware_id` was `hardware_id` until decision 47 (2026-09-07, `tasks/core/020`): this route served the probe-read ID as `probe_hardware_id` and the self-reported one as `hardware_id`, and a caller comparing `hardware_id` from here against `hardware_id` from `/probes/enrolled` got a near-miss byte-swap rather than a category error. `409` if a study is in flight |
| `GET` | `/studies` | — | `{keep, studies: [{study_id, study_name?, status, started_utc_ms?, ended_utc_ms?, steps?, taps?, note?}]}` — every study still on disk, **newest first, bounded by the retention count in force** (`EMBARCH_STUDY_RESULTS_KEEP`, default 50), which `keep` reports so a caller can say what the list is a list *of*; `keep: 0` means retention is off and this is everything. The one route that makes a past study reachable without having kept its id. `status` is `pending`/`running` from the live job registry, `completed` for a finalized `events.json`, `failed` where the registry is still there to say so, and **`interrupted` for an `events.json.partial` with no job behind it** — decision 69, never `completed` and never `failed`. `steps` is a tally, `{total, passed, failed, timed_out, unknown}`, not the steps themselves (`/study/{id}/steps` serves those); `taps` is `[{name, encoding, rendered, named?, timed?, self_excluded?, source_deferred?}]`, the same facts `/study/{id}/streams` carries minus the file bookkeeping. **`steps` and `taps` are absent, not empty, when they could not be read** — "ran no steps" and "we could not read its steps" are opposite facts — and `note` says in prose what went wrong. A disk read throughout, cached on `(path, mtime, len)` per directory, so a repeated listing is a `stat` per study rather than 50 parses |
| `POST` | `/study` | `Study` (JSON), plus `?allow_version_mismatch=1` and `?flashed_firmware_version=<string>` | `202 {study_id, status: "accepted"}`. `409` if a study is running, **or** on the version gate (`requires.dev_bench_version` against what the bench reported, before `StudyStart` is sent, so no step runs). `400` on a `steps_crc`/`streams_crc`/`protocols_crc` mismatch — each named separately — a protocol `validate_protocol` refuses, a `RunProtocol` naming a protocol or entry state the study does not carry, a tap naming an undeclared signal, or a blank `requires`. `502` for dev-bench link failures specifically. `study_id` is a random hex string, not a UUID |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}` | — | `{status: "pending"\|"running"\|"completed"\|"failed", current_step?, total_steps?, result?, reason?}`. **`current_step` is the 0-based index of the last step that *finished*** — absent until one has, so `"step {current_step} of {total_steps}"` reads one short at the end and a caller wanting "2 of 2" adds one itself (decision 43). It is **usually** `total_steps - 1` on a completed run and **that is a consequence, not an invariant**: nothing checks it before reporting `completed`, so a step whose result was lost — which decision 40 explicitly permits — completes lower, and a zero-step study reports `total_steps: 0` with no index at all. The leading clause holds in both cases; do not derive completion from this field. `result` is `events.json` read off disk on demand — never a resident copy (`StudyResult` is ~1.3 MB by type). **A study the in-memory registry has forgotten is served from disk instead** (decision 71): a finalized `events.json` answers `completed` with its `result`, an `events.json.partial` answers `interrupted` with no result and a note, and `current_step`/`total_steps` are absent rather than reconstructed. `404` now means only what it says — no job **and** no results directory, so a study that never existed and one this Core has forgotten are distinguishable, which the pre-decision-71 blanket `404` claimed was impossible. On a `"failed"` from an early stop, `reason` takes one of three shapes and they are not interchangeable (decision 45): a step that failed is named with dev-bench's own reason in parentheses, a step that timed out is named and said to have timed out, and *"the StepResult saying which step failed did not arrive"* means no step outcome was recorded at all — a lost frame, not a timeout |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/events` | — | SSE. One JSON `StudyEvent` per frame (`StepCompleted`/`SampleBatch`/`GattTranscript`/`StreamText`/`StatusChanged`), pushed as Core processes it, filtered to this `study_id`. **`StreamText` is one chunk off a `Text`-encoded tap** — `{stream_id, stream_name, step_index, rx_utc_ms, text}` — carried verbatim, with no line framing: a chunk can split a line *and* a UTF-8 character, and assembling lines is the consumer's job (decision 70). `Raw` taps push nothing, deliberately. A frame is bounded by `MAX_STREAM_CHUNK_BYTES` (512). A slow subscriber gets `event: lagged` rather than silently missing messages. **No `Last-Event-ID`, no replay** (decision 41): a reconnect starts at "now," with no way to ask for what it missed — `GET /study/{id}` is the authoritative record a caller falls back to |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/steps` | — | `{study_name?, timed, steps: [{index, step_name, outcome, reason?, delay_before_ms?, started_utc_ms?, ended_utc_ms?}]}`. A disk read, so a study outlives the process that ran it. `timed` is false for a pre-2026-08-27 study and for an empty list, deliberately. `outcome` is a bare `"Pass"`/`"Fail"`/`"TimedOut"` with `reason` beside it, not `Outcome`'s tagged JSON |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/streams` | — | `{streams: [{id, name, encoding, rendered, note?, named?, timed?, self_excluded?, source_deferred?}]}` — what the taps captured and, the reason this route exists, **why a trace has no names when it has none**. `rendered` is a boolean, not a file name: a caller never opens the file. `named`, `timed` and `self_excluded` are the three independent booleans a caller branches on instead of pattern-matching `note`'s prose; `source_deferred` is a fourth, set for a tap declared against a source this bench has no front end for (currently `PowerFrontEnd`, `embarch-dev-bench` decision 24) — Core states this, it does not measure it (decision 63) — see [result-layout.md](result-layout.md) |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/stream/{name}` | `?raw=1` | One tap's capture as bytes. By default the rendered file where the encoding has one (`text/csv`); `?raw=1` serves the `.bin`. A tap with no rendering serves raw either way; a `Text` tap comes back `text/plain`. `404` if the study declared no such tap (listing the ones it did) or if it captured nothing. Names resolve through `streams/index.json`, so only a declared name resolves to any file |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/stream/{name}/arrivals` | — | One tap's **arrival sidecar**: embarch-core's own receipt times for the bytes of a capture that carries none of its own. Two encodings have one, each keyed by the only coordinate its bytes have — an `OutpostTrace` tap by **frame index** (`frame_index,rx_utc_ms,frame_bytes`, which Core already joins into the rendered CSV itself), and a `Text` tap by **byte offset** (`byte_offset,core_rx_utc_ms,bytes`). The `Text` one is what this route exists for: a console's raw file *is* its rendering, so there is no rendered row to append `core_rx_utc_ms` to, and without this a console read back off disk is bytes with no times — unplaceable on any shared axis. Served as CSV bytes. `404` if the study declared no such tap, if the tap keeps no sidecar (naming its encoding), or if it captured nothing |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/stream/{name}/load` | — | An `OutpostTrace` tap's load repartition: per-subject shares plus the coverage line, computed once here from the same rendered CSV `/stream/{name}` serves as bytes ([suite decision 4](../../suite/decisions.md); [decision 62](../decisions.md)). `{unit, window_extent, gap_extent, gap_fraction, records_lost, has_time_base, thread_extent, idle_record_extent, isr_extent, unaccounted_extent, below_resolution_spans, subjects: [{key, label, unnamed, kind, entries, measured_spans, total_extent, share, excluded_spans, excluded_extent, gap_crossing_spans, open_ended_spans, open_started_spans, below_resolution_spans}]}`, plus `rows`, `rows_dropped_by_cap`, `row_cap` and `rows_unparsed` alongside it. `400` if the tap is not an `OutpostTrace` — there is no timeline in a `Samples`/`GattTranscript`/`Raw`/`Text` tap to repartition. `404` if the study declared no such tap, or the tap has not rendered (no manifest applied yet, or the render failed — same cases `/streams`'s own `note` reports). `422` if the rendered CSV's column list does not match this build's `outpost::csv_header()` — inherited from [`embarch-ui` decision 10 (trace)](../../embarch-ui/decisions/trace-view.md)'s pin, carried across rather than re-implemented loosely |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/stream/{name}/load/spans` | — | The same tap's decoded per-lane timeline `/load` reduces into the summary above and then discards — served here undigested (decision 65, `decisions/stream-index.md`; decision 64 decided this should ship). `{unit, t_from, t_to, records_lost, rows, rows_dropped_by_cap, row_cap, rows_unparsed, gaps: [{from, to, records_lost, cycle_span, frame_index, row_index, unbounded_start}], lanes: [{key, label, unnamed, kind, spans: [{from, to, open_start, open_end, crosses_gap, below_resolution}]}]}`. `t_from`/`t_to` are the capture window's bounds in `unit`s, absolute — unlike `/load`'s `window_extent`, which only carries their difference — and every `Span`/`Gap`'s `from`/`to` is stamped against them. `Gap`'s five extra fields are full parity with `embarch-ui`'s own `Gap` (decision 66); axis-health diagnostics and point events are not served here or anywhere, permanently (decision 66). Same tap resolution, same `400`/`404`/`422` cases as `/load`, from the same underlying decode (not a second one) |
