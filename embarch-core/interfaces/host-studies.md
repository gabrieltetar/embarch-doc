# embarch-core interfaces: Host studies

**Status:** active, 2026-10-26.

Index: [../interfaces.md](../interfaces.md). Rationale: [../decisions/host-study.md](../decisions/host-study.md) (decision 95), [../decisions/host-prompt.md](../decisions/host-prompt.md) (decision 105, prompts). Reads back through the Studies routes ([studies.md](studies.md)) unchanged.

| Method | Path | Body / Query | Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| `POST` | `/host-studies` | `HostStudy` (JSON, `embarch-study-designer`) | `202 {study_id, status: "accepted"}`. `400` for a study that fails its own checks, an `Instrument` that is not declared, or a `streams` tap that is not `Text` + `WholeStudy` on a declared direct signal, has an `Exchange` timeout over 10,000 ms or a `Prompt` timeout over 3,600,000 ms, or names a signal that is undeclared or not writable; the body names the list and step. `409` while any study holds `study_lock`, a tap's port is held by a capture or another tap or cannot be opened, or a lease is live without its token. `422` for an unknown field. |
| `POST` | `/study/{id}/prompt` | `{prompt_id, answer: "confirmed"\|"cancelled", answered_by: "ui"\|"mcp"\|"cli"}` | `200 {study_id, prompt_id, answer, answered_by, status: "recorded"}`; the step ends within 20 ms. `409` for a `prompt_id` that is not the one waiting (the body names the one that is), a prompt already answered, a running study with no prompt waiting, or a study not running; `404` for an unknown id; `422` for any other `answer` or `answered_by`. **Not lease-gated** (decision 105). |
| `GET` | `/study/{id}/line-counts` | — | `200 {study_id, line_counts: [{name, tap, pattern, total, tap_lines, repeats: [{list, repeat_index, name, passes: [{pass, value?, count}]}], outside_passes}]}`: the stored study's `line_counts` over its finished run's raw tap files (decision 99). `404` for a study with no `study.json`; `409` before it ends. |
| `POST` | `/study/{id}/line-counts` | `{line_counts: [LineCount]}` | The same, for these counts: a corrected pattern over the same run. `400` for a tap the study does not declare or a pattern that does not compile. Nothing is stored. |
| `POST` | `/study/{id}/cancel` | — | `202 {study_id, status: "cancelling"}` for the running host study; the step in flight finishes, the rest are skipped, the teardown runs. `409` for a bench study or one not running; `404` for an unknown id. |

**`GET /study/{id}/steps` rows of a host study** carry, beyond the bench fields: `list` (`Steps`|`Teardown`), `detail` (`marker_seen`, `empty_reply` = zero bytes back, `until_not_seen`, `refused`, `port_lost`, `port_unavailable`, `exchange_failed`, `waited`, `cancelled`, and for a `Prompt` `confirmed`, `prompt_cancelled`, `prompt_timed_out`), `note?`, `reply` (raw, capped), `reply_truncated`, `reply_bytes`, `elapsed_ms`; a `Prompt` row also `prompt_id`, `prompt_deadline_utc_ms` and `answered_by` (`ui`/`mcp`/`cli`, null when nobody answered). `GET /study/{id}` returns `events.json` with `completed`, `stopped_reason`, `teardown_failures`, `iterations`, `repeats`, and while a prompt waits, `pending_prompt {prompt_id, list, step_index, step_name, text, opened_utc_ms, deadline_utc_ms}`. A step in a `Repeat` also carries `iteration {list, repeat_index, pass}` and `inner_index`. SSE adds `HostStepCompleted`, `PromptPending` (the `pending_prompt` fields, flat) and `PromptResolved {prompt_id, answer: confirmed|cancelled|timed_out|study_cancelled, answered_by, resolved_utc_ms}`.

**Taps (decision 98).** Each `streams` tap is a capture opened at submission and held until the teardown ends; its files are `streams/<name>.txt` and `.arrival.csv` (read through the stream routes), and `events.json`'s `streams` lists per tap `id`, `name`, `signal`, `port`, `state` (`stopped` normally), `bytes_written`, `truncated`, `gaps`.
