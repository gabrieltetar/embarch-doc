Two more of the same kind: dev-bench's step ceiling had silently forked from the crate's (16 vs 64, reconciled by nothing), and two open-questions bullets were still describing shipped features as unbuilt.

- 2026-08-24 — **New root-level doc, registered in §6: [embarch-stream-pipeline-proposal.md](embarch-stream-pipeline-proposal.md)** — a proposal (not accepted, and deliberately not written into any `design.md`) to collapse the suite's three near-identical capture pipelines into one. Opened by a direct question — "is there a pipeline to get the NUS logs during a study?" — whose answer is no: `serial_log` was never a DUT-console path ([embarch-decision-reversals.md](embarch-decision-reversals.md) row 11), and `GattMonitorAll`'s `gatt_activity` is capped at 32×512 bytes inline in the ~1.3 MB `StepResult` two docs already carry crash reports against. Two findings worth the timing: nothing has ever streamed a real sample, so the reshape is nearly free until roadmap Milestone 4 makes the current shape load-bearing; and the design removes bytes from `StepResult` rather than adding any.
