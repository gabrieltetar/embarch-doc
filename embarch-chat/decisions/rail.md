## Rail and mark

### 94 — A rail on the chat's left edge holds Usage, Memory, Promptu, Library, Suggestions and the side question

Decided by the owner, 2026-10-29 and 30 (`embarch-promptu` decision 16). Under the header, which keeps the pane's width (91), a column 30 px wide runs down the chat's left edge, folded to its icons as VS Code's activity bar is; the pointer on it unfolds their names over the timeline, to 140 px, never moving it. Usage (99) sits alone at the top, as it is about the account; Memory (98), Promptu (95), Library, Suggestions, the 💬 side question and the settings gear (107) at the bottom, beside the input they work on. **Library and Suggestions left the input's bar for it** (amends 38), and **the 💬 did too** (EMB-59): the same enable rule, toggle and Esc as decision 57, `aria-pressed` since it is a mode and opens nothing, its answers panel still above the input; their menus still open over the input, and the 💡's swaps still preview in the draft (37, 86). What the rail opens itself — Promptu's card, the Memory menu — sits beside it, and the rail stays folded while one is open. The column (91) centres in what is right of the rail. A read-only chat has no input and no rail. It is a toolbar named "Chat tools": ↑/↓, Home and End move between its entries. **Each entry is a one-colour inline SVG in `currentColor`** (bars, brain, sparkle, books, bulb, speech bubble), so it follows the theme's foreground; unlit is dimmed by opacity, lit is full strength (Promptu in the accent, Suggestions in the waiting colour, Usage amber or red), never a CSS filter. The owner first picked colour emoji (`embarch-promptu` decision 40) and changed it 2026-11-09 (EMB-56), the one coloured spot left beside decision 54's mark; the promptu side follows in `tasks/promptu/003`. Their place at the bottom stands, and Usage alone at the top his answer to fork P16, 2026-10-30. **Rejected:** colour emoji with filters for their states; Library, Suggestions and the 💬 staying in the input's bar; a rail that pushes the timeline when it unfolds; Usage atop the others at the bottom, as first built.

### 54 — "EmbArch" at the right of the status bar starts a chat, in the mark

A one-colour icon font (the E cut back a pixel from the A, edges snapped to the 16 px grid, generated by `tools/make_glyph.py`); the same shape is the sidebar icon, and a chat's editor tab shows the two-colour mark, one file per theme. **Rejected:** a codicon, the thin letters (the E fades at 16 px).
