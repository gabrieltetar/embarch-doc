### 119 — The linker offers a study's captures and traces

**Decided 2026-11-26** (`embarch-promptu` decision 16; EMB-415; the eighth and ninth kinds, `src/assistant/refsStudies.ts`, over 116's runner). "Compare it with yesterday's trace" and "the VBUS capture from the soak run" name things Core retains and the linker never saw. **Read with `embarch-api --json list-studies --limit 20`** through `ApiRunner`, **under the one rule 116 states for every verb**: run in the chat's folder, a 10 s limit, the answer or the failure kept 5 minutes per folder and verb, run only before a cards call, no binary no spawn, a failure logged once per cause. The verb's text, `list-studies --limit 20`, is the cache key, so `list-instruments` and `list-studies` each run once inside the five minutes and a failure of one leaves the other's answer alone; the runner splits the text into arguments and nothing else of it differs by verb. **One entry per tap of each listed study**, newest study first (the verb sorts them): kind `trace` when the tap's `encoding_kind` is `outpost_trace`, else `capture`. A signal's live capture is no kind. A study whose `taps` is null, or a tap with no name or encoding, offers nothing and breaks nothing; a study id that cleaning would change or that holds a space is not offered. **Offered** by the source's own match, since the entries hold no words for 112's scorer: (1) a **study id** in the draft offers its taps with no cue word (112's exact name); (2) otherwise the draft must carry **capture, trace, recording, stream, tap, study, run, samples, log** (whole word, any case, with their plurals), and a tap is offered when the draft shares a significant word with the tap's name or its study's name (the cue words left out of both, as 114 does), most shared first and newest first among equals, **or newest first alone when the draft carries last, latest, yesterday or today**; (3) a draft that shares no word but carries trace or traces is offered the newest four traces, and one that carries capture, recording, stream, tap or samples the newest four taps. Study, run and log open the gate for a match on a name only: "run the tests" offers nothing. **Written** `trace "<tap>" of study <id> "<study name>" (OutpostTrace, <YYYY-MM-DD>)` and `capture "<tap>" of study <id> "<study name>" (<encoding_kind>, <YYYY-MM-DD>)`, with ` not rendered` added when the tap's `rendered` is false; the date is the study's start in UTC and is left out with none; each name through `clipTitle` at 60 characters and carried in `titles`, so `copiesTitle` drops a card that copies a run of it into a draft, as 117 does for a document's title. **Assumed, not measured, the owner's to strike:** the 20-study limit, the four-tap fallback, treating study, run and log as name-only cues and the UTC date. **Not seen against a real Core**; tested on a fake `embarch-api` (`test/fake-api.mjs`) with three studies, one with null taps. **Rejected:** a scorer entry per tap (a bare "trace" matches no word of any), offering the newest tap for "run" or "log" (every draft about running anything would show one), a signal's live capture as a kind, the tap's step counts or size in the line.
