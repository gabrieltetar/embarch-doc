# embarch-chat decisions: The linker's references

**Status:** active, 2026-11-25.

What the 💡's cards link a vague phrase to: the desk's reference index (88) and the sources and kinds the linker takes (112). The assistant that asks Haiku is [assistant.md](assistant.md) (26, 27).

Index: [../decisions.md](../decisions.md). Current truth: [../spec.md](../spec.md).

### 88 — The chat mirrors its desk's reference index: a card names the real issue or PR, and one in Claude's prose is a link

A vague "the widget PR" got a card asking which PR [measured on a synthetic case: 6 of 6, 3.9 s median]. **The host mirrors the window's desk's index** (`embarch-desk` decision 56) — `GET /refs` for the desk `GET /view` is asked for, read once and again only when the view's `refs_ms` moves, handed whole to each chat page and never the token (20). **Before the cards call the draft is matched locally**: its significant words against the titles, a word few titles share counting most, plus any key, `owner/repo#N` or `PR #N` in the chat's own repo it names; the chat's own item is left out, its card already in the prompt (27). The best lines — an issue with up to two PRs naming it, or a PR alone; at most 8, about 600 tokens — go in a `<desk_references>` block of the cards prompt only (amended 2026-11-25, 112: the block is `<references>`, 12 lines and 3,600 characters, and takes other kinds): the same single call (26), nothing on a keystroke, ghost text untouched. **The reference is written by the extension, never by Haiku**: Haiku names a line by its id and the vague phrase exactly as the draft has it, and the replacement comes from the index — `PR owner/repo#7 "Widget" (ABC-12)`, or the issue first when the phrase says issue, ticket or card — spliced in as written (a `$` in a title is text) at the phrase's first whole-word occurrence, a combining mark counting as part of its word. Anyone can title a PR on a repo in the desk's scope, so a title is data: stripped of `<`, `>`, line breaks and what shows nothing, clipped at 90 characters, and any other card that would carry four of a title's words in a row into the draft is dropped [measured on the synthetic case: a replace naming the PR 8 of 8, 2.34 s median; a title written to steer the cards copied 0 of 4]. Several lines fitting give a card each, at most 3; none, a bracketed question. **In Claude's prose** `ABC-12`, `owner/repo#7` and `PR #7` in the chat's own repo are links only when the index knows them, as atlas citations are only with an atlas (16): an issue opens Linear, a PR GitHub, and the hover names it in full, `owner/repo#7 "Widget" · merged`, so a wrong one shows before the click. A bare `#7` never is — "step #3" is no PR. The chat's own repo — read from its folder's git config, regular files of 64 KB or less, as `gh` picks one with no terminal — is used for that `PR #7` and the draft matcher only, never for a step (87). **Rejected:** a resolve route on the desk called per card (Linear and GitHub on the cards' path), Haiku calling the desk's tools (a round-trip in a call stripped of tools, 26), feeding it only the desk's cards (a card holds its own item's PRs, and a Done issue never on a card was invisible), Haiku writing the reference, a bare `#N` linked because one PR in the index has it.

### 112 — The linker takes references of any kind: sources, one block, one matcher

**Decided 2026-11-25** (`embarch-promptu` decision 16, build step 5; its `docs/contract.md` 15.1–15.2, forks L1–L22; amends 88's block and caps, 88 otherwise standing). The linker reads **sources**, one file each (`src/assistant/refsDesk.ts` is the first), so each later kind (project, document, branch, worktree, section, symbol, node, instrument, capture, trace) is one more source and a row here, not a change to the matcher. A source gives entries `{kind, key, words, names, line, text(find)}`; the desk's issues and PRs are the first source, their line and written text exactly as 88 has them (a PR whose issue the index has still rides on that issue's line, first among its PRs). **One matcher over every source**: the entries the draft names (`names`, or a source's own reading of the draft) come first, then those whose words match, scored within their kind (a word's rarity counts among that kind's entries, so thousands of symbols do not drown a PR) and merged best first; issues and PRs share one pool, as 88 scores them, each kind keeping its own tag. **The block is `<references>`**, at most 12 lines and 3,600 characters (`spec/constants.md`), at most 4 lines of one kind before the best of the rest fill it, each line `[r3] <kind>: <line>`. **A gated source** (it lists cue words) offers by words only when the draft carries one of them, whole word in any case; an entry the draft names exactly is offered with none, alone. Issues and PRs are not gated. **The rule names only the kinds present**: its nouns, its "points at an issue or a PR" and its example come from the kinds in the block, so a draft with only PR lines sends the prompt it sent before but for the block's name and the tags. Haiku still names a line by id and the vague phrase; **`referenceText` asks the entry** (`text`), never Haiku, and `copiesTitle` checks every entry's titles, so a title written to steer the cards still reaches no draft. One "References" submenu holds every kind. **Assumed, not measured:** the 12-line and 3,600-character caps and the per-kind 4 are the plan's recommendations (`embarch-promptu` forks L10, L11, L16), which the owner may strike; the cue words are each kind's unit's. **The recorded `cards-refs` call is not re-recorded** (no real Haiku call in this unit): it replays by the prompt's TASK line, so it still applies, and a later unit records it against the new prompt. **Rejected:** a second block per kind, a matcher per kind, one pool for every kind (issues and PRs would drown thousands of symbols).

### 113 — The linker offers the chat folder's branches and worktrees

**Decided 2026-11-25** (`embarch-promptu` decision 16, build step 5; contract 15.2's `branch` and `worktree` rows; EMB-410; the first kind added as 112 says, `src/assistant/refsGit.ts`). **One source, read from git in the chat folder's main clone**: `git for-each-ref --sort=-committerdate --count=300` over `refs/heads` and `refs/remotes/origin`, a remote branch with a local twin once, `origin/HEAD` none; and `git worktree list --porcelain`, each with its path and branch (`deskService.ts`'s `git`, `mainRepo` and worktree read, shared). **Gated**: by words only when the draft carries branch, worktree, checkout, clone, rebase or merge (whole word, any case); a branch name or worktree path the draft names exactly is offered with none (112). An entry's words are its name split on `/`, `-`, `_` and `.`; a worktree's also its folder's. **Written**: `branch <name>`, plus ` (PR owner/repo#N)` when the desk's index holds a PR on that branch (the chat's own repo's first); `worktree <absolute path> (branch <name>)`. **The chat's own branch and worktree are left out**, as 88 leaves out its own item; a name or path that would change if cleaned (an unseen character, `<`, `>`, past 200 characters) is not offered rather than offered cut. **Refresh**: before a cards call, only when the folder's last read is over 60 s old, so git runs at most once a minute while drafts change, and never for ghost text or a keystroke (26); a read that fails leaves both kinds empty, is not retried inside the minute, and logs once per folder. **Assumed, not measured:** the 300 cap, the cue words and the 60 s are `embarch-promptu`'s recommendations, which the owner may strike. A bare `main` the draft names offers the branch with no cue word, as 112 has an exact name do. **Not seen against a real window**; tested on scratch repos. **Rejected:** a git read per keystroke, a branch offered without a gate (every draft with "main" or "fix" would show it), the PR's title on a branch line (the branch is what is written).


### 114 — The linker offers the atlas's datasheet and spec sections

**Decided 2026-11-25** (`embarch-promptu` decision 16, build step 5; contract 15.2's `section` row; EMB-411; the second kind added as 112 says, `src/assistant/refsAtlas.ts`; amends 16 not at all: it adds no prose link). **One source, read with `embarch-atlas refs --json`** (`atlas_refs`, `atlas/109`) in the chat's folder: one entry per section of every translated document, its words the title's and the id's, naming its document's `doc_id` and `part`; the binary is the new `embarchChat.atlasPath` setting, `embarch-atlas` on PATH by default. **Gated**: by words only when the draft carries datasheet, spec, specification, standard, section, chapter, table, manual, errata or app note (whole word, any case), or names a document's part, which opens that document's sections alone; those cue words are left out of an entry's own words, so "datasheet" matches no title. **Written** `<doc_id> p<page> §<id> "<title>"`: the `<doc_id> p<N>` head is the shape 16's links match, so the same text is a citation link wherever the chat finds the atlas; a section with no page, a page past 9999 or an id that cleaning would change is not offered. **Refresh**: a stat of the atlas's `current.json`, `atlases/` and `docs/` before a cards call, and the verb runs only when one moved (or the binary did), so two cards calls with no change spawn it once; 10 s limit. No binary, no atlas or a failed run leaves the kind empty and logs once per cause. **Assumed, not measured:** the cue words, the 10 s limit and the change-stat refresh are the plan's recommendations (choices 11, 12, 14, 15), which the owner may strike; the 20,000-section cap is this unit's. A change inside one document's folder that leaves `docs/`'s own mtime alone is not seen until something else moves. **Not seen against a real atlas**; tested on a fake `embarch-atlas` (`test/fake-atlas.mjs`). **Rejected:** a verb run per cards call, a prose link of its own (16 stands), the verb's symbols and nodes here (EMB-412 shares this read).
